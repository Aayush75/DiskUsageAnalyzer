# Community conduct

Treat users and contributors with respect. Keep discussions constructive, explain disagreements with evidence, and make room for different levels of technical experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation. When the repository is published, use its private reporting route or contact a maintainer privately for conduct concerns; do not post another person's sensitive information publicly. This policy applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.
